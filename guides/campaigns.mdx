Tokenz Campaigns let you run promotional programs to reward your players. Create gift code campaigns where players enter a code to claim a reward, or free item campaigns where players can claim rewards on a schedule — all managed from the Tokenz Dashboard.

## Campaign types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gift Codes" icon="ticket" href="/guides/gift-codes">
    Players enter a campaign code to receive a reward. Validate eligibility, redeem the code, and fulfill via webhook.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free Items" icon="gift" href="/guides/free-items">
    Players claim a reward without a code, on a repeating daily, weekly, or monthly schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Campaign statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | In progress; redemptions not allowed |
| `scheduled` | Queued; becomes active at `startAt` |
| `active` | Live; redemptions and claims succeed |
| `paused` | Manually paused; redemptions rejected |
| `ended` | Finished; no further redemptions (irreversible) |

## Campaign management

Campaigns are created and managed entirely in the [Tokenz Dashboard](https://dashboard.tokenz.one). When configuring a campaign, you control:

- **Active periods** — set a start and optional end date to schedule when redemptions are accepted.
- **Reward items** — choose the SKU and quantity players receive when they redeem or claim.
- **Per-player limits** — cap how many times a single player can redeem within the campaign.
- **Total limits** — set a global maximum number of redemptions across all players.
- **Refresh cadence** — for Free Item campaigns, choose daily, weekly, or monthly to automatically reset per-player limits each period.

## Fulfillment webhook

Both Gift Codes and Free Items trigger the same `redemption.completed` webhook event when a redemption or claim succeeds. Follow this shared fulfillment pattern in your webhook handler:

1. **Verify the `Tokenz-Signature` header** before granting any reward. Reject requests that fail signature verification.
2. **Deduplicate on `redemptionId`** — Tokenz may deliver the same event more than once. Store fulfilled `redemptionId` values and skip re-processing.
3. **Check `kind`** to distinguish `CODE_REDEMPTION` (Gift Code) from `FREE_ITEM_CLAIM` (Free Item) if your handler needs to branch.
4. **Grant the reward** using `reward.skuRedemptionReward.sku` and `quantity` to identify what to deliver to the player.
